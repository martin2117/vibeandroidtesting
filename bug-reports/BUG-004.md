# [BUG-004] [Cart] Faulty Percentage Discount Calculation Divides by 1000.0 Instead of 100.0

## Environment
- **Build**: Jetpack Compose & React Native (Broken build)
- **Device/OS**: Android 14 (API 34) on Pixel 7 Emulator
- **Detected By**: Maestro, Appium, Espresso, UI Automator
- **Target Element**: `discount-input`, `apply-discount`, `order-total`

## Steps to Reproduce
1. Launch app and log in with valid credentials (`demo@techshop.com` / `password123`).
2. Add Product 1 ($60.00) twice or Product 1 ($60.00) + Product 2 ($90.00) (Subtotal: $120.00 or $150.00).
3. Navigate to Cart screen (`tab-cart`).
4. Enter discount code `SAVE10` into the discount input (`discount-input`).
5. Tap "Apply" button (`apply-discount`).

## Expected Result
A 10% discount must be deducted ($12.00 on a $120.00 subtotal), yielding a final order total of "Total: $108.00".

## Actual Result
The discount formula divides by `1000.0` instead of `100.0` (`vm.subtotal * 10 / 1000.0`), deducting only $1.20 (1%) instead of $12.00 (10%).

## Severity & Impact
- **Severity**: Major
- **Rationale**: Direct financial calculation error applying incorrect discount amounts to customer orders.

## Scope & Blockers
- **Cross-Build Behavior**: Reproduces on both Jetpack Compose (`AppViewModel.kt`) and React Native (`CartContext.js`).
- **Blocks**: None.
